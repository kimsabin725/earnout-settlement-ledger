Earnout Settlement Ledger — demo

A complete earnout lifecycle on Canton: four parties, rules locked at signing, and an attempt to rewrite them that fails on the ledger rather than in a courtroom.

59 seconds · captions burned in · recorded against LocalNet. The DevNet re-recording is listed as Season 3 work in docs/s3-log.md.

What you are watching

  1. Signing. Buyer proposes metric definitions, thresholds, evaluation logic, attestor and arbiter. Seller accepts. Both signatures are required for the contract to exist.
  2. A clean quarter. The bank attests the period's account snapshot; the buyer reports against it; the contract evaluates and records the earned amount.
  3. A contested quarter. An anomaly routes to the arbiter named at signing — not to a forum chosen after the argument started.
  4. The attack. The buyer tries to move the thresholds after a bad quarter. There is no such choice in the model, so the transaction fails.
  5. Settlement. The final payout transfers atomically in a single transaction.
