Earnout Settlement Ledger · a two-minute check

Is your earnout arithmetic, or judgement?

Some earnouts settle themselves: a number clears a line, the money moves. Others need somebody to decide — whether the effort was reasonable, whether that expense belonged in the calculation, whether the milestone counts. Only the first kind can be settled by a contract that was signed before anyone knew the answer. Eight questions tell you which kind yours is, and if it is the second, exactly which part needs a human.

Nothing you enter leaves your browser. The check runs entirely in this page: no server, no analytics, and the page makes no request of its own once it has loaded — the only thing your browser fetched was the page itself. View source if you would rather confirm that than take my word for it. No figures are asked for either: the answer depends on the shape of the clause, not its numbers.

The refusal rules here are the same ones in replay/compile.mjs, which is what actually compiles a clause file and replays it on the ledger. Five clauses from the public record have been through it — one reproduces the payout that was actually made, four are refused, and the reasons are in docs/validation.md.

If it got your clause wrong, that is the most useful thing you could send me. Reply to whatever brought you here, or open an issue. The verdict line is all I need — no figures, no names, no deal.

What comes back gets written down in docs/validation.md, including replies that say this page is wrong, and each one becomes a test case so the same mistake cannot come back. If nobody replies, that is recorded too.
