Earnout Settlement Ledger · a two-minute check

Would your earnout clause compile?

This model puts an earnout on a ledger where the metric, the target, the threshold and the arbiter are signed once at closing and cannot be changed afterwards. It only fits some clause shapes. This tells you in two minutes whether yours is one of them — and if not, exactly why.

Nothing you enter leaves your browser. The check runs entirely in this page: no server, no analytics, and the page makes no request of its own once it has loaded — the only thing your browser fetched was the page itself. View source if you would rather confirm that than take my word for it. No figures are asked for either: the answer depends on the shape of the clause, not its numbers.
The thing that decides whether money is owed.
A bank, an auditor, or a paying agent attesting the number — not the buyer's own statement.
The refusal rules here are the same ones in replay/compile.mjs, which is what actually compiles a clause file and replays it on the ledger. Five clauses from the public record have been through it — one reproduces the payout that was actually made, four are refused, and the reasons are in docs/validation.md.

If this got your clause wrong, that is the most useful thing you could tell me — open an issue or reply wherever you found this. The verdict line is all I need; no figures.
